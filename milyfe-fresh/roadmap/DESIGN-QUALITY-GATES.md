# MiLyfe Design Quality Gates

**Status:** Design-phase review standard

## Gate 1 — Coherence

- No duplicate authority
- No unexplained OS
- No branch conflict
- Terms are consistent
- MiLyfe naming is uniform

## Gate 2 — Human value

- A real person can understand the benefit
- The feature solves a real problem
- The feature does not depend on hype
- The feature has an exit and support path
- The feature does not hide costs or risks

## Gate 3 — Authority

- Owner and steward identified
- Scope defined
- Human approval defined
- Delegation and revocation defined
- External authority boundary defined

## Gate 4 — Data

- Data owner identified
- Purpose defined
- Consent defined
- Retention defined
- Export and deletion defined
- AI and third-party access defined

## Gate 5 — Economic truth

- Projected and settled states separated
- Fees visible
- Rewards verified
- Reserves identified
- No negative balance behavior
- Refund and dispute path defined

## Gate 6 — Security and safety

- Threats identified
- Secrets separated
- Least privilege defined
- Failure behavior defined
- Incident path defined
- Child and vulnerable-person risks reviewed

## Gate 7 — Accessibility and inclusion

- Keyboard and screen-reader path
- Contrast
- Reduced motion
- Language support
- Low-bandwidth behavior
- Mobile behavior
- Human support

## Gate 8 — Contract and integration

- API, event, receipt, permission, and data contracts exist
- Versioning exists
- Cross-branch handoff defined
- Offline behavior defined
- Rollback defined

## Gate 9 — Publication

- Claims sourced
- Assets owned or licensed
- Private data removed
- Security findings classified
- Public status accurate
- Review approvals recorded

## Gate 10 — Build authorization

No implementation begins until Gates 1–9 pass for the relevant scope. A design may be approved for the next design phase without being approved for production.
