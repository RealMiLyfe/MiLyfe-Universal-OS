# MiLyfe Completion and Activation Register

**Status:** Master readiness register

## Design complete when

- Design package `01–31` is present
- Constitution and Mi Being Charter are reviewed
- Authority matrix is approved
- Brand specification is approved
- Founder story is supplied and reviewed
- API/event/receipt contracts are registered
- Legal and financial review questions are assigned
- Security threat model exists
- Agent steering files exist
- Phase gates are approved

## Repository ready when

- GitHub repository exists
- Protected branches exist
- Root steering files are installed
- Agent roles and skills are installed
- Issue and PR templates are installed
- Secrets policy is active
- Security scanning is active
- Release evidence path exists

## Build ready when

- Contracts are versioned
- Test harness exists
- Local environment works
- Synthetic data fixtures exist
- Backup and restore are tested
- Kernel vertical slice is approved

## Pilot ready when

- Onboarding works
- Business onboarding works
- MiMarket sandbox works
- MiForge useful offer works
- Support is staffed
- Security evidence passes
- Accessibility passes
- Legal review covers active functions
- Money states are accurate
- Locked rails remain locked
- Export, deletion, recovery, and rollback work

## Public rails ready when

- USD and `$MLY` structure is reviewed
- Partners are approved
- Reserves and reconciliation exist
- Consumer terms exist
- Fraud and sanctions controls exist
- Financial and tax treatment is documented
- Human approval is recorded
