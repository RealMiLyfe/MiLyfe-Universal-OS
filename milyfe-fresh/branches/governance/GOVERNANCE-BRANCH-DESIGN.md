# MiLyfe Governance Branch Design

**Status:** Focused design phase · V2-aligned 2026-09-21 (see `../../FOUNDATION-DELTAS-V2.md`)
**Primary OSes:** Governance OS, MiLegal, MiResolve, MiJustice

## 1. Branch purpose

The Governance Branch protects people, creates accountable rules, resolves disputes, provides legal access, and defends rights.

## 2. Governance OS

Owns:

- Proposals
- Participation
- Amendments
- Stewardship
- Treasury oversight
- Community decisions
- Constitutional review
- Anti-capture controls

Does not own courts, clinical care, `$MLY` issuance, or election results.

## 3. MiLegal

Owns:

- Legal information
- Contract workflows
- Jurisdiction profiles
- Professional routing
- Legal holds
- External relationship records

Does not pretend that AI summaries are legal advice or that internal rules override external law.

## 4. MiResolve

Owns:

- Internal disputes
- Service disputes
- Marketplace disputes
- Corrections
- Appeals
- Restorative processes

Does not absorb criminal, constitutional, civil-rights, court, incarceration, or public-office authority.

## 5. MiJustice

Owns:

- Rights defense
- Serious accountability
- Court support
- Appeals
- Redress
- Restitution
- Government accountability

## 6. Branch contracts

Governance receives trunk identity, context, data, capability, event, receipt, and security services. It returns decisions, approvals, cases, appeals, records, policy changes, and public explanations.

## 7. Branch risks

- Governance capture
- AI decision substitution
- Unequal access
- Retaliation
- Confidentiality failure
- Legal overclaim
- Mixing campaign and public-office records
- Unreviewed emergency authority

## 8. Design gates

- Authority matrix approved
- Due-process lifecycle complete
- Case and appeal contract complete
- Legal-source provenance complete
- Accessibility complete
- Human review roles assigned
- External legal boundary documented
