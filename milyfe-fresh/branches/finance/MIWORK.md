# MiWork — Finance Branch (Phase 6)

**Status:** `READY_FOR_REVIEW`. **Plain value:** find real work, prove real skills, get paid real $MLY — with warnings when a "job" smells like exploitation.
**Sources:** Finance design §5 · Manual MiWork/MiTask, Wren/Jo helpers, quests, contributions, Vanguard/coords (peace jobs), wage warnings.

- **Purpose:** work opportunities, contributions, skills, jobs, gigs, apprenticeships, service work, contributor records, work agreements, livelihood pathways. (Finance/Lifestyle bridge per document map.)
- **Users:** workers, employers/shops, apprentices, mentors, co-ops, labor clinics.
- **Owns:** opportunity board (jobs/gigs/apprenticeships/service; neighbors-first, then town); skill + contribution records (verified completions, EAS-style badges for spotter/sweeper-class work); work agreements (scope, pay, schedule, safety, termination — plain language); payout claims (verified work → MiMoney settlement); apprenticeship tracks; livelihood pathways (Learn→Work: credential→opportunity matching); wage/exploitation warnings (Wren: flags below-norm pay, unsafe terms, misclassification smells — routes to labor clinic, never legal verdict).
- **Does not own:** employment legal status (no hidden employment claims); payroll tax filing (exports + routing); ledger; credential issuance.
- **Entities + data:** `opportunities` · `skills` (verified) · `agreements` · `work_records` (hours, outputs, verifications) · `payout_claims` · `pathways`. Recruitment alone is never counted as earned work; rewards need verified contribution.
- **Permissions:** post needs shop/org verification; agreements need dual signature; payouts need verification + approved rules; youth work fenced by MiChildGate + jurisdiction profiles (no child labor, ever).
- **Contracts:** `work.post/apply/agree/verify/claim/pathway/warn` + handoffs to money (payouts), edu (credentials), market (gig fulfillment), place (hire boards), legal (labor-law routing), resolve (work disputes).
- **Receipts:** applications, agreements, verifications, payouts, warnings, pathway milestones.
- **Offline:** boards cached; hour logs signed on-device; claims queue; verifications sync later.
- **Security:** job-scam detection; exploitation pattern flags; youth-work fencing; co-op fraud checks.
- **Accessibility:** plain agreements; audio apply; skill-first (not résumé-first) matching; human job coaches.
- **Support:** worker support desk; "I wasn't paid" fast path (verify → claim → resolve); labor-clinic routing.
- **Legal:** labor-law routing per jurisdiction; no misclassification facilitation; apprenticeship compliance; youth labor prohibitions.
- **Metrics:** placements, verification rates, payout accuracy/time, warning precision, pathway completion, worker retention/earnings growth.
- **Failure recovery:** unpaid verified work → claim enforcement path; bad match → re-match + remedy; agreement breach → resolve + rebook.
- **Export:** full work + earnings + skill history exportable, even during dispute.
- **Removal:** opportunities close with applicant notice; agreements end with settlement checklist; records archived per retention.
- **Fork behavior:** work records stay in cell; skills portable as signed credentials; cross-cell gigs via bridge + settled payouts.
- **Activation gate:** synthetic worker runs apply→agree→log-hours→verify→payout→warn→export incl. offline hour-log + unpaid-claim drill; gates 1,2,4,5,6,7,8 pass.
