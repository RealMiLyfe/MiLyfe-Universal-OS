# MiMoney — Finance Branch (Phase 6)

**Status:** `READY_FOR_REVIEW`. **Plain value:** one honest ledger. Your $MLY is real, never negative, every move signed by you and receipted. No bank, no hidden fees, no fake balances.
**Sources:** Finance design §4 · Money-State Contract · V2 rail spec · Constitution Art VII · Manual Part Two ($MLY framework, safeguards, issuance).

- **Purpose:** the sole authoritative $MLY ledger — balances, postings, settlement, rewards, treasury interfaces, reserves, refunds, reversals, reconciliation, financial receipts.
- **Users:** members (Pocket), shops (tills), circles/places (treasuries), stewards (oversight), auditors.
- **Owns:** ledger + ChartOfAccounts-equivalent posting rules; value-state machine (projected/pending/settled/rewarded/disputed/reversed); transfers (atomic `transfer_mly`: row-locked, positive-only, no self-send, sufficient-balance, receipt); crypto intake (6 assets, confirmation policies, reorg handling); cash intake (trusted nodes, dual confirmation, limits); P2P swap settlement (dual confirmation); card authorization/capture (MiPay digital NFC/BLE/QR + offline store-carry-forward signed payloads with device-key sequence + settlement double-spend checks); rewards engine (verified contribution + approved rules only); treasury operations (explicit authority, circuit breaker, runway gauge); reserves + reconciliation; refunds/reversals via due process.
- **Does not own:** exchange operation, custody of others' money (your credits, your keys, your export), $MLY sales (no ICO/sale/pre-mine beyond issuance rules), price-setting, investment pitching (no APY/yield/appreciation promise), USD conversion/redemption/custody automation (locked).
- **Entities + data:** `accounts` (entity, pots) · `postings` (balanced entries, sum 0, no account <0) · `crypto_deposits` (asset/amount/tx/confirmations/policy/status/credited/receipt) · `cash_exchanges` (node/member/fiat/mly/dual-confirm/limits/receipts) · `swap_listings` (maker/offer/terms/meeting/status/receipts) · `finance_cards` (entity/form-factor/key-ref/limits/status/receipts) · `rewards` (contribution-proof/rule/payout) · `treasury_ops` (authority/budget/spend) · `reserves` · `reconciliations`. Pots (display): Weekly/Thanks/Place. Issuance split default: 70% place / 30% commons. Earn caps/week anti-whale.
- **Permissions:** every mutation human-signed or human-approved-policy-executed; ledger-rule change = P + H (+MiLegal H); treasury allocation = P + H (+MiLegal H); USD-rail enable = H×(human, MiLegal, MiMoney); AI never moves money alone.
- **Contracts:** `money.transfer/deposit-crypto/deposit-cash/list-swap/settle-swap/card-auth/card-capture/reward/treasury-spend/refund/reverse/reconcile` + handoffs from forge (welcome budgets), market (order settlement), work (payouts), gov (treasury oversight), resolve/justice (freeze/reverse/restitution).
- **Receipts:** every posting, deposit, exchange, swap, auth, reward, treasury move, refund, reversal, reconciliation — receipted with state labels.
- **Offline:** Offline Pocket — signed offline payloads (device-key sequence, caps, expiry) carried via MiDTN; settled on reconnect with double-spend + sequence checks; conflicts to human review (MiWalk: money never auto-resolved).
- **Security:** no-deprivation invariant (no debit below zero, ever); safety freeze (leave-now) available; 34% treasury circuit breaker → 48h cooldown + 80% supermajority; sybil/farming defenses (personhood + caps + duplicate review); card key lifecycle (issue/replace/revoke; device keys never leave device).
- **Accessibility:** one number balance + pot breakdown; animated send + celebration; plain receipts; human treasurer help.
- **Support:** treasurers (Nia + human), "where did my $MLY go?" tracer, dispute entry to MiResolve.
- **Legal:** community-credit characterization pending Phase 8 qualified review; Doorways rule (public cash-out operators follow MSB/MiCA/local rules themselves); tax export; fraud/sanctions controls; consumer terms before any fiat-touching feature.
- **Metrics:** circulation, velocity, pot distribution, deposit/swap volumes, reward payouts, treasury runway gauge (spend rate vs reserves with honest "months left" display), reconciliation breaks (must be zero), farming blocks.
- **Failure recovery:** chain reorg → deposit re-verification (credit only when policy re-passes); double-spend attempt → reject + quarantine + review; settlement outage → queue + honest pending labels + catch-up reconciliation.
- **Export:** full transaction history exportable anytime, even during disputes.
- **Removal:** accounts close with zero-balance verification + history archive; cards revoked with receipt; listings canceled with notice.
- **Fork behavior:** ledger does not fork balances (each cell settles its own; cross-cell value moves are explicit settled transfers with lineage); forks start with own issuance under own authority.
- **Activation gate:** deposit→transfer→swap→card-auth→reward→treasury-spend→refund→reconcile drill incl. offline-pocket + breaker-trip + zero-reconciliation-break; gates 1–9 pass (Econ truth + Security + Publication fully evidenced).
