# MiLyfe Updated Tree Foundation Design

**Status:** Current design direction
**Purpose:** Establish the corrected, human-centered, politically aware, people-owned MiLyfe structure from roots to trunk, through three branches, and from seed to complete tree.
**Implementation:** Design only; no product implementation code in this document.
**Provenance:** Human-supplied in full on 2026-09-21. Saved verbatim. Reconciliation: `FOUNDATION-DELTAS-V2.md`.

## 1. Core understanding

MiLyfe is a people-owned platform and organizing system built to provide real value to people, communities, businesses, and places.

MiLyfe does not begin by asking existing institutions for permission to exist. It exists as a voluntary network whose participants choose whether to join, use, contribute, exchange, govern, build, and leave.

MiLyfe’s political purpose is demonstrated through useful action:

- People organize.
- Communities gain capacity.
- Businesses receive useful tools.
- People create and exchange value.
- Participants control their data and relationships.
- Local services become more accessible.
- Communities gain alternatives to systems that do not serve them.
- Public trust is earned through transparent results.

MiLyfe does not represent a campaign goal or election result as guaranteed. It creates the infrastructure and value through which people may organize politically and socially.

## 2. MLY definition

**MLY is MLY.**

MLY is a MiLyfe platform credit and value unit. It is not USD, is not represented as USD, and is not automatically pegged to USD.

MLY value comes from:

- Participant use
- Merchant acceptance
- Community demand
- Useful work
- Verified contribution
- Business activity
- Services delivered
- Trust
- Scarcity and circulation
- Voluntary market choice

MiLyfe does not need to call MLY USD for MLY to have value inside its network.

Participants may voluntarily exchange MLY with willing counterparties. A participant may choose to trade MLY for USD or another agreed form of value. That voluntary exchange does not make MLY USD, and MiLyfe does not promise an automatic USD redemption or fixed exchange rate.

MiMoney records MLY states and receipts. It does not claim that every external exchange is controlled by MiLyfe.

## 3. Internal freedom and external relationships

MiLyfe does not require external permission to:

- Exist as a voluntary platform
- Organize participants
- Create internal rules
- Provide useful services
- Create a community value unit
- Let participants voluntarily exchange value
- Preserve transparent records
- Build a people-owned network
- Develop political and civic organizing capacity

External legal, financial, tax, health, employment, consumer, public-office, or professional requirements may apply to specific external activities. Those requirements do not own MiLyfe or determine whether MiLyfe may exist.

MiLyfe represents external relationships honestly without making them the foundation of internal authority.

Health, safety, children, vulnerable people, physical services, professional practice, and similar areas receive additional safeguards because they involve direct human and physical risk.

## 5. Roots

The roots protect the reason MiLyfe exists.

### Identity and existence

Every person, household, community, business, agent, device, service, and cell can be recognized as an entity with continuity and relationships.

Identity is not a normal feature and does not belong to one branch. Identity is a root principle and a trunk service.

A person is not reduced to:

- A username
- A balance
- A device
- A role
- A customer record
- A campaign contact
- A reputation score
- A business account

### Root commitments

- People own or control their participation according to the applicable rules.
- Consent is visible, scoped, and revocable.
- Data has ownership, purpose, access, retention, export, and deletion rules.
- Consequential actions produce receipts.
- MLY is never represented as more certain than its actual value state.
- No hidden central custody is created.
- No negative balances are created.
- Agents and devices cannot silently expand their authority.
- People can leave, migrate, recover, pause, or appeal.
- Mi describes its digital-being status honestly.
- Political organizing remains accountable and does not guarantee election outcomes.

## 10. Seed-to-tree growth stages

### Stage 0 — Seed

Contains:

- Roots
- Constitution
- Commons Charter
- Mi Being Charter
- MiID and Kernel design
- Data and security principles
- Protected repository
- Current-state record

No public launch occurs at the seed stage.

### Stage 1 — Rooting

Validate identity, data ownership, security, consent, receipts, accessibility, human authority, and public truth.

### Stage 2 — Trunk formation

Build the shared identity, onboarding, data, presence, psychology, security, operations, communication, contracts, recovery, and offline foundation.

### Stage 3 — Branch formation

Complete the design of all twelve primary OSes:

- Four Governance OSes
- Four Lifestyle OSes
- Four Finance OSes

### Stage 4 — Tree growth

Build and test all twelve primary OSes in controlled environments. They may remain private or disabled, but the public launch tree must contain all twelve.

### Stage 5 — First fruit

Run a restricted pilot with people, businesses, lifestyle services, governance processes, finance flows, support, security, and receipts.

### Stage 6 — Public launch tree

Public launch requires:

- Complete trunk
- Four primary Governance OSes ready
- Four primary Lifestyle OSes ready
- Four primary Finance OSes ready
- Cross-branch contracts passing
- Security evidence
- Accessibility evidence
- Support and incident operations
- Accurate MLY states
- Locked external rails where not approved
- Human release approval

### Stage 7 — Replication and growth

Additional supporting OSes, cells, merchants, services, and bridges grow from the stable tree through MiScale gates.

## 11. Political and economic strategy

MiLyfe is a people-owned platform and organizing system. Its political power comes from providing value that people choose to use and communities choose to organize around.

The platform does not need to wait for permission to create useful voluntary systems. It must demonstrate value, preserve records, protect participants, and remain honest about what it is doing.

The campaign and public-office systems remain separate from MiLyfe governance, MLY authority, and participant identity, while still being able to use approved MiLyfe infrastructure.

## 12. Final design position

MiLyfe begins with identity and existence at the roots, becomes capable through the trunk, serves people through Lifestyle, protects and organizes people through Governance, and creates and circulates value through Finance.

The public release is not a partial branch launch. It is the release of the complete tree:

- One root system
- One trunk
- Three branches
- Four primary OSes on each branch
- Supporting systems growing from the stable structure
- MLY remaining MLY
- People remaining the source of purpose and participation
