# MLY Definition — Roots (Foundation V2 §2 + §9 boundary)

**Status:** Current design direction (human-supplied 2026-09-21, `../UPDATED-TREE-FOUNDATION-DESIGN.md` §2, §5, §9).
**One line:** MLY is MLY.

## Definition

MLY is a MiLyfe platform credit and value unit. It is not USD, is not represented as USD, and is not automatically pegged to USD.

## Where MLY value comes from

Participant use · merchant acceptance · community demand · useful work · verified contribution · business activity · services delivered · trust · scarcity and circulation · voluntary market choice.

MiLyfe does not need to call MLY USD for MLY to have value inside its network.

## Voluntary exchange

Participants may voluntarily exchange MLY with willing counterparties. A participant may choose to trade MLY for USD or another agreed form of value. That voluntary exchange does not make MLY USD, and MiLyfe does not promise an automatic USD redemption or fixed exchange rate.

## Recording vs control

MiMoney records MLY states and receipts. It does not claim that every external exchange is controlled by MiLyfe.

## Honesty rules (roots-level)

- MLY is never represented as more certain than its actual value state (projected/pending/settled/rewarded/disputed/reversed — always labeled).
- MiLyfe does not need permission to design or use MLY internally as a voluntary platform credit. MLY must still be described honestly, recorded transparently, and never represented as USD or guaranteed redemption.
- This definition does not remove external gates: where outside law applies to a specific activity (licensing, finance, payments, health, campaigns, employment, taxes, consumer protection, data handling), the preserved rule governs: "MiLyfe's internal constitutional foundation must be preserved. External legal, financial, tax, professional, and jurisdictional review remains an activation gate for activities that require it."
