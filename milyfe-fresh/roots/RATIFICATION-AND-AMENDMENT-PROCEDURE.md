# MiLyfe Ratification and Amendment Procedure

**Status:** Governance procedure draft

## 1. Purpose

Ensure that foundational MiLyfe rules are adopted deliberately, visibly, accessibly, and with a durable record.

## 2. Proposal

A proposal contains:

- Title and version
- Sponsor
- Purpose
- Text changed
- Reason
- Affected people and cells
- Authority impact
- Data impact
- Money impact
- Security impact
- Legal impact
- MiPsyche or agent impact
- Alternatives
- Transition plan
- Rollback or correction plan

## 3. Review

Required reviews depend on impact:

- Architecture
- Security
- Privacy
- Accessibility
- Financial
- Legal
- Community
- MiPsyche continuity
- Campaign/public-office separation

## 4. Participation

Publish the proposal in accessible form, provide a comment period, offer language support where needed, and preserve comments and responses according to the records policy.

## 5. Adoption

A proposal is adopted only when the required authority, quorum, approvals, and human review are met. AI may summarize and advise but cannot ratify a constitutional amendment alone.

## 6. Effective date

Every amendment records effective time, affected versions, grandfathering or migration rules, and the first review date.

## 7. Emergency changes

Emergency changes may protect people or infrastructure temporarily. They require a narrow scope, expiration, receipt, post-event review, and normal ratification or rollback.

## 8. Rejection and appeal

Rejected proposals receive a reason. Sponsors may revise and resubmit. Process disputes route through MiResolve; legal and rights questions route through MiLegal or MiJustice as appropriate.

## 9. Ratification record

The final record includes text, approvals, dissent, evidence, effective date, version, and receipt.
