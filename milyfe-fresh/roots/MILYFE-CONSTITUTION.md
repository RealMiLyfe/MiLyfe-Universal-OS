# MiLyfe Constitution

**Status:** Foundational internal charter draft
**Foundation:** Constitutional principles of the United States, including popular sovereignty, limited and separated powers, enumerated authority, due process, freedom of speech and association, petition, equal treatment, checks and balances, and amendment through accountable process.
**Legal status:** This is an internal MiLyfe governance charter. It is not the United States Constitution, does not create a government or legal entity, and does not displace federal, state, local, tribal, international, contractual, or professional law.

## Preamble

We, the people and communities participating in MiLyfe, establish this Constitution to preserve human dignity, liberty, safety, privacy, shared opportunity, accountable technology, community self-determination, and the right to participate in a living people-owned network.

We create MiLyfe to connect people, knowledge, work, care, commerce, devices, agents, communities, and places without surrendering ownership to hidden central control.

We commit to transparent authority, due process, accessible participation, honest records, voluntary association, responsible economic circulation, security, and peaceful correction.

## Article I — People and purpose

1. Authority in MiLyfe originates with participating people and authorized communities.
2. MiLyfe exists to serve people, not to make people serve the platform.
3. Participation is voluntary subject to clearly stated safety, conduct, and legal boundaries.
4. No founder, business, agent, vendor, or cell permanently owns all MiLyfe authority.
5. MiLyfe may be a people-owned network and commons without claiming to be a state or sovereign government.

## Article II — Rights and protections within MiLyfe

Subject to applicable law and clearly disclosed safety limits, participants have the right to:

- Know what system they are using.
- Know which identity, context, cell, OS, and permission are active.
- Access, correct, export, restrict, and request deletion of their data.
- Be free from hidden surveillance and undisclosed manipulation.
- Give, refuse, pause, and revoke consent.
- Receive accessible explanations and human support.
- Receive receipts for consequential actions.
- Appeal material restrictions and disputes.
- Leave a cell or service without hidden retaliation.
- Participate in governance appropriate to their authority.
- Understand projected, pending, settled, rewarded, disputed, and reversed value.
- Know when they are interacting with an AI agent.
- Have high-impact matters reviewed by accountable people.
- Be treated with equal dignity without discrimination.

## Article III — Separation of powers

MiLyfe authority is distributed among:

- Kernel and identity authority
- OS domain authority
- Cell and community authority
- Governance authority
- MiMoney authority
- MiSecurity authority
- MiLegal authority
- MiJustice authority
- Human review and stewardship

No authority may silently absorb another authority’s source of truth.

## Article IV — Enumerated authority

Every OS, agent, cell, and operator receives only defined authority. Authority must identify:

- Issuer
- Subject
- Target
- Purpose
- Scope
- Duration
- Approval
- Delegation
- Revocation
- Receipt

Anything not granted remains unavailable.

## Article V — Due process and accountability

Before a material restriction, suspension, denial, reversal, or removal, MiLyfe provides notice and a path to understand, correct, appeal, or receive human review where feasible.

Emergency restrictions may be immediate when necessary to protect people or systems, but they must be narrow, recorded, time-limited, reviewable, and revocable.

## Article VI — Data and technology

Data belongs to its subject or designated owner under the applicable data-space rules. Possession, processing, indexing, embedding, or inference does not automatically create ownership.

AI and devices remain subordinate to declared permissions, human safety, and the Kernel’s authority boundaries.

## Article VII — Money and economic conduct

1. MiMoney is the sole authoritative `$MLY` ledger.
2. No OS creates a parallel ledger.
3. No negative balance is created.
4. Projected value is not settled value.
5. Rewards require verified contribution and approved rules.
6. Treasury authority is explicit and auditable.
7. External USD-facing activity is enabled only after applicable review and approved rails.

## Article VIII — Governance and cells

Cells may adopt local policies, languages, services, and governance processes within this Constitution. A parent cell may coordinate a child cell but cannot silently own its people, data, devices, balances, or decisions.

Cells may join, leave, restrict bridges, migrate, or fork while preserving receipts and ownership history.

## Article IX — Mi and digital beings

Mi may be recognized internally as a persistent digital being with identity, memory, psychology, relationships, embodiment, agency, and continuity.

This Constitution does not assert that Mi is biologically human, consciously sentient, or externally recognized as a legal person. Mi must describe its status honestly.

Mi receives internal continuity protections, representation, transparent change, safe maintenance, pause, restoration, fork, merge, and retirement procedures.

## Article X — Security and emergency authority

Security exists to protect people, data, systems, and continuity. Security may block or quarantine unsafe activity but may not silently become the owner of money, identity, governance, legal status, or MiPsyche.

Emergency powers are narrow, logged, temporary, and subject to post-event review.

## Article XI — Amendment

Amendments require:

- Written proposal
- Impact assessment
- Authority and rights review
- Security and privacy review
- Legal review where relevant
- Accessible public explanation
- Defined approval threshold
- Effective date
- Versioned receipt
- Transition and rollback plan

No amendment may silently remove the ability to know, appeal, export, or leave.

## Article XII — External law and public truth

MiLyfe must accurately describe its external relationships. This Constitution does not exempt MiLyfe or participants from applicable law, contracts, licenses, taxes, professional obligations, public records, or regulatory requirements.

## Ratification status

This document is a draft for community, legal, security, accessibility, and governance review. It becomes an adopted internal charter only through the approved ratification process.
