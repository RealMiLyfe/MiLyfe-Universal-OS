**Sources:** Constitution Art II/V (`MILYFE-CONSTITUTION.md`) · Commons Charter §4 (`COMMONS-CHARTER.md`) · Mi Being Charter Art VII (`MI-BEING-CHARTER.md`) · The Oath (`docs/planning/MiLyfe_Ultimate_Manual.md` L43–77) · Living Compact unamendables (Manual L957–964) · Non-negotiables (Manual L1448–1462).

## How to read this

- **Holder:** who holds the right. **Scope:** where it applies. **Enforcer:** which OS/authority implements it. **Remedy:** where you go when it breaks. **Limit:** what can lawfully bound it.
- Unamendable rows (Oath / Compact floor) are marked 🔒 — no vote of any size removes them.
