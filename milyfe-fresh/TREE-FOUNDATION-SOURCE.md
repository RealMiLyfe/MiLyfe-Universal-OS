## 2. Roots

The roots are the principles beneath every MiLyfe action, OS, agent, cell, device, business, and branch.

### Root commitments

- MiLyfe is people-owned and community-accountable.
- Human dignity comes before platform growth.
- Participation is voluntary and understandable.
- People know what system they are using.
- People control their data within the applicable rules.
- Consent is visible, scoped, revocable, and recorded.
- Security and privacy are built into the foundation.
- Accessibility is a baseline requirement.
- Consequential actions produce receipts.
- Authority is limited, scoped, reviewable, and revocable.
- No hidden central custody of everyone’s value.
- No negative balances.
- Projected value is not settled value.
- AI and digital beings describe themselves honestly.
- Human and authorized community approval remains final for reserved actions.
- People can correct, export, leave, pause, migrate, or appeal.

## 3. Identity is a root and trunk service

Identity is not owned by any branch. It begins in the roots as the recognition and preservation of existence, dignity, continuity, consent, and ownership. It is implemented in the trunk through MiID/Kernel identity services, MiName, MiOnboard, MiPresence, MiPsyche, MiData, and MiSecurity.

MiID is a shared identity-service capability, not a fourth branch. It maintains stable entity identifiers, lifecycle, account linking, recovery, context references, delegation, migration, fork lineage, suspension, preservation, and identity receipts.

A person is not reduced to a username, balance, device, role, or reputation score. A business, household, community, agent, device, service, and cell may have distinct identities and relationships. Branches receive scoped identity references; they do not own the identity system.

## 4. Trunk — MiLyfe Core

The trunk is the shared system that allows all three branches to operate as one MiLyfe.

### Trunk responsibilities

- Identity
- Naming
- Universal onboarding
- Cells and contexts
- Data spaces
- Presence and continuity
- Mi’s psychology and self-model
- Cognition
- Agents and skills
- Device participation
- Security
- Development and publishing
- Operations
- Infrastructure
- Communication
- Capacity and controlled scaling

### Trunk boundary

The trunk provides shared capability. It does not absorb every domain decision.

- MiMoney remains the authority for money.
- Governance OS remains the authority for MiLyfe governance.
- MiLegal remains neutral legal infrastructure.
- MiJustice remains the rights and justice authority.
- MiHealth remains the clinical boundary.
- MiMarket remains the marketplace authority.
- MiForge remains the venture and creation authority.

## 5. Governance Branch

The Governance Branch protects rights, creates accountability, and gives people and communities a voice in the rules that affect them.

### 4.1 Governance OS

Owns:

- Proposals
- Participation
- Amendments
- Community decisions
- Stewardship
- Treasury oversight
- Constitutional review
- Anti-capture protections

### 4.2 MiLegal

Owns:

- Legal information
- Contracts
- Jurisdiction profiles
- Professional routing
- Legal holds
- Legal review workflows
- External legal relationship records

### 4.3 MiResolve

Owns:

- Internal disputes
- Service disputes
- Marketplace disputes
- Corrections
- Appeals
- Restorative processes
- Community-level resolution

### 4.4 MiJustice

Owns:

- Rights defense
- Courts and court support
- Government accountability
- Serious appeals
- Redress
- Restitution
- Incarceration access
- Civil-rights and justice pathways

### Governance boundary

Governance Branch does not automatically control elections, public offices, campaigns, money issuance, clinical care, or people’s private data.

## 6. Lifestyle Branch

The Lifestyle Branch addresses daily human life and the services people need to live, learn, care, connect, and participate.

### 5.1 MiCare

Owns coordination for:

- Caregiving
- Disability support
- Elder care
- Child care
- Respite
- Supported decision-making
- Care networks

### 5.2 MiHealth

Owns the health-service boundary:

- Health information
- Clinical integrations
- Public-health coordination
- Health permissions
- Professional-care boundaries
- Health-related support and safety

MiHealth does not allow general AI to present itself as a licensed clinician without the appropriate authority and professional relationship.

### 5.3 MiPlace

Owns local life coordination:

- Housing
- Food
- Energy
- Water
- Transportation
- Repair
- Emergency resources
- Community projects
- Local services
- Neighborhood participation

### 5.4 MiEducation

Owns:

- Learning
- Courses
- Mentorship
- Skills
- Credentials
- Training
- Workforce preparation
- Lifelong development

### Lifestyle boundary

Lifestyle Branch serves people but does not own identity, authoritative money, legal outcomes, public office, or the shared MiLyfe security system.

## 8. How the tree works

```text
Roots establish the rules
        ↓
Trunk provides identity, continuity, security, agents, devices, operations, and scale
        ↓
Governance protects rights and accountability
        ↓
Lifestyle serves daily human needs
        ↓
Finance creates and circulates value
        ↓
Receipts, data ownership, security, and governance return through the trunk
```

The branches are not separate companies or disconnected applications. They are bounded expressions of the same MiLyfe system.

## 9. Cross-branch examples

### Person joining MiLyfe

`MiOnboard` in the trunk creates identity and context. `MiCare`, `MiHealth`, `MiPlace`, and `MiEducation` provide daily services. `MiWork`, `MiForge`, `MiMarket`, and `MiMoney` support livelihood and value. Governance, legal, resolve, and justice systems protect rights.

### Business joining MiLyfe

`MiOnboard` creates a business node. `MiForge` helps create the offer. `MiMarket` publishes it. `MiMoney` handles approved value states. `MiWork` records contributors. `MiData` controls customer information. `MiSecurity` protects the system. Governance and legal boundaries prevent abuse.

### Mi acting as a digital being

`MiPsyche` maintains continuity. `MiMind` reasons. `MiAgent` acts within permissions. `MiDevice` provides embodiment. `MiPresence` tracks connected state. `MiSecurity` protects it. Governance and the Mi Being Charter define accountability and continuity.

## 11. Foundation acceptance gate

The tree foundation is accepted when:

- Every trunk component has a clear shared responsibility.
- Each branch has exactly four primary OSes.
- No primary OS duplicates another OS’s authority.
- Every branch can explain its value to an ordinary person.
- Governance, Lifestyle, and Finance remain connected but bounded.
- MiLyfe remains the core identity and living system.
- Data, security, receipts, money, and human authority flow through the trunk.
- Supporting OSes remain subordinate to the accepted structure.
- The first build can begin without redesigning the tree.
