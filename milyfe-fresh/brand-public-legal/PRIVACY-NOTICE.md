# Privacy Notice (Tree V1 draft) — Phase 8

**Status:** `READY_FOR_REVIEW` as draft; legal sign-off PENDING. **Sources:** root `PRIVACY.md` · Constitution Art VI · MiData design.

- **Local-first:** your data lives on your devices, encrypted; the commons holds the minimum needed to operate.
- **Classes:** public / neighbors / household / private / sealed — each with purpose-bound, expiring, revocable sharing you can preview ("what can this person see?").
- **Never:** hidden surveillance, undisclosed manipulation, sale of data, health/biometrics leaving your private store without your explicit slice consent, youth data exposed to strangers.
- **AI:** no training on your private data without explicit consent; personal on-device learning stays on-device unless you choose otherwise.
- **Your controls:** access, correct, export, restrict, delete — one-tap export, always available, even mid-dispute.
- **Safety:** abuse-report + leave-now paths; sealed records (child reports, sealed appeals, witness recordings) access-logged.
- **Breach:** 90-day coordinated disclosure intake; honest breach notices per jurisdiction duties.
