# AI Policy (Public) — Phase 8

**Status:** `READY_FOR_REVIEW`.

- Mi and named helpers are labeled AI — never a person, lawyer, or police. Always logged.
- AI assists and drafts; humans approve reserved actions (money, identity exceptions, legal, safety, governance decisions, MiPsyche changes, pauses).
- On-device first: core Mi runs offline on your device; cloud escalation only with your explicit consent, re-vetted locally.
- Helpers never farm dependency, isolate you, exploit grief, target children emotionally, or coerce money/access/loyalty.
- You can mute/stop any helper instantly, see what it can do, audit what it did, and always reach a human.
- No training on private data without consent; your personal model is exportable and deletable.
