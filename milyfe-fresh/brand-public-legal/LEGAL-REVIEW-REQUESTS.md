# Risk, Disclosure & Partner Review Requests — Phase 8 (questions for qualified humans; opinions NOT fabricated)

**Status:** `OPEN` — human supply. These questions inform risk, disclosure, partner, health, safety, tax, professional, and jurisdiction-specific decisions for EXTERNAL touchpoints. They are not permission gates for MiLyfe's existence, internal rules, or internal voluntary MLY activity (including pilots of internal flows). Fiat-touching activations additionally need partners + reserves evidence + consumer terms + controls + human approval.

## Outside risk/disclosure review requested (external touchpoints)

1. Entity/posture: commons with no central legal person — exposure map for stewards, node operators, doorway businesses.
2. $MLY characterization (security/commodity/currency/money-transmission/property/barter) + state-by-state notes starting with Florida.
3. MSB/money-transmitter analysis for: trusted-node cash intake, community P2P swaps, own-card issuance (digital + plastic), UBI/rewards.
4. Consumer protection: disclosures, refunds, fee rules, marketing claims review (Gate 9).
5. Youth privacy (COPPA + state), education/health privacy boundaries, mandatory-reporting interaction.
6. Accessibility law duties; lawful-process/caleA-class data duties; tax-reporting duties (income/info-return characterization).
7. Terms/privacy/AI/device/marketplace drafts: enforceability + conflict check vs applicable law.
8. IP: AGPL-3.0 posture, fork duties, trademark (MiLyfe/Mi/$MLY), asset licensing.

## Outside financial risk review requested (external touchpoints)

1. Reserves: design, custody, attestation cadence, reconciliation standards.
2. Accounting treatment: issuance, UBI, rewards, swaps, fees, treasury ops; tax treatment notes.
3. Fraud/sanctions/AML program for intake/swap/card flows (thresholds, monitoring, SAR-adjacent duties, geographic controls).
4. Circuit breaker + runway gauge adequacy; earn-cap economics; 70/30 split effects.
5. Partner review framework (node operators, ATM hosts, card manufacturers, auditors).
6. Consumer-funds handling (even "no custody" claim must be verified against each flow).

## Activation rule

No USD-facing rail, reverse automation, exchange listing, fiat POS, or plastic issuance activates until: risk/disclosure/partner review complete + reserves evidenced + consumer terms final + fraud/sanctions controls live + reconciliation proven + human approval recorded. Internal MLY activity (ledger, transfers, rewards, UBI, welcome, participant P2P) is never gated on outside permission.
