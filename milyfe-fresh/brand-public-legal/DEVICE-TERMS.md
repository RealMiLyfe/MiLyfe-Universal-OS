# Device Terms — Phase 8

**Status:** `READY_FOR_REVIEW` as draft; legal sign-off PENDING.

- Your device, your node: participation (compute/relay/sense) is opt-in per workload class, pausable in one tap, and metered honestly.
- Contribution rewards follow approved rules and settle only via MiMoney; grid-failure multipliers where policy defines.
- Actuation (anything affecting the physical world) is always gated — two-person gate or pre-registered life-safety playbook — never autonomous.
- Radio/spectrum/drone operation follows the lawful subset in your jurisdiction profile; illegal operation is disabled, not hidden.
- Lost/stolen: panic freeze kills sessions, hides location, freezes jars. Shared devices: per-person vaults, kiosk timeouts, neutral notifications.
