# Public Terms (Tree V1 draft) — Phase 8

**Status:** `READY_FOR_REVIEW` as draft; legal sign-off PENDING (Phase 8 human-supplied opinion).
**Sources:** root `TERMS.md` (adopted + tree-aligned) · Constitution · Oath · Manual non-negotiables.

1. **What MiLyfe is:** a people-owned voluntary commons (not a government, state, bank, court, hospital, law firm, or police). Internal governance is not a substitute for legal rights; applicable law governs conflicts.
2. **One signup, one profile**, free forever for rights, learning, emergencies. No sale of accounts; no buying rights.
3. **Your data:** you own it; consent is visible, scoped, revocable, recorded. Export always works, even during disputes. Leaving: export → close, no retaliation.
4. **$MLY:** community credit; real from day one; voluntary peer exchange; MiLyfe runs no exchange, holds no custody, makes no investment promises, sets no prices (see `MLY-DISCLOSURE.md`).
5. **Conduct:** no harm, no CSAM, no crime planning, no sanctions evasion, no scams, no impersonation, no retaliation for good-faith reports. Youth: no adult DMs, no public location, MiChildGate always on.
6. **Due process:** notice + hearing + appeal + human review for material restrictions; emergencies narrow + time-limited + reviewable.
7. **Helpers/AI:** labeled, logged, limited; AI never decides alone on reserved actions; pause is human-only.
8. **Open source:** AGPL-3.0; forks must publish changes; the 24 Oath principles travel with every fork and cannot be removed.
9. **Changes:** terms change only via ratified process with notice + transition plan; rights to know/appeal/export/leave can never be removed.
