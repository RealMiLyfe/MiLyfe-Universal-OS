# Architecture Blueprint (Public) — Phase 8

**Status:** `READY_FOR_REVIEW`. Plain-language public companion to the trunk/branch designs. No security-sensitive internals.

MiLyfe is one tree: **Roots** (the rules: Constitution, Charters, Oath 24 — power stays with the people) → **Trunk** (shared systems: one signup, your data under your control, security, offline-first connections) → **three Branches**: Governance (votes, legal help, disputes, rights defense), Lifestyle (care, health boundaries, your street, learning), Finance (start earning, marketplace, honest $MLY ledger, work). One signup, one profile. Free forever for rights, learning, emergencies. Works when the internet fails. Your keys, your data, your export — always, even in a dispute. Open source (AGPL-3.0): anyone can read, run, and fork it; the Oath travels with every fork.
