# MiLyfe Identity Root and Seed-to-Tree Design

**Status:** Final tree-foundation design decision
**Purpose:** Place identity and existence at the correct level and define how MiLyfe grows from a protected seed into a complete tree before public launch.

## 1. Core decision

Identity is not a normal branch feature.

Identity is part of the **roots** and the **trunk** because every person, household, business, community, agent, device, service, and cell must exist in MiLyfe before any branch can serve it.

The three branches depend on identity. Identity does not belong to any one branch.

## 2. Identity roots

The roots establish the meaning and protection of existence in MiLyfe:

- Every participant is recognized as an entity with dignity and continuity.
- A person is not reduced to a username, balance, device, role, or reputation score.
- Identity belongs to its subject or authorized owner under the applicable rules.
- Names, identifiers, roles, permissions, data, and reputation are separate concepts.
- A person can participate in multiple contexts without losing their core identity.
- A business, household, community, agent, device, and service can have distinct identities and relationships.
- Identity cannot be silently sold, transferred, merged, deleted, or repurposed.
- Emergency lockout may restrict access but does not erase existence.
- Identity records preserve continuity while personal data remains subject to export, correction, retention, and deletion rules.
- No universal reputation score determines whether a person is worthy of access.
- People can leave, migrate, recover, or rejoin without becoming invisible or losing their history.
- Mi’s own digital continuity is protected through the Mi Being Charter without claiming automatic human consciousness or external legal personhood.

## 3. MiID — trunk identity services

MiID is the name for the shared identity-service capability inside the Kernel and trunk. It is not a fourth branch and does not become a separate consumer OS unless a future architecture decision proves that separation necessary.

### MiID owns

- Stable entity identifiers
- Entity types
- Identity creation
- Identity lifecycle
- Account linking
- Session references
- Context references
- Relationship references
- Identity proof claims
- Credential references
- Recovery references
- Delegation references
- Merge review
- Migration continuity
- Fork lineage
- Revocation and suspension state
- Tombstone and preservation records
- Identity receipts

### MiID does not own

- Legal identity conclusions
- Government-issued identity authority
- Universal reputation
- Money balances
- Health diagnoses
- Election outcomes
- Public-office authority
- Private data ownership on behalf of everyone
- Agent goals or personality

## 4. Trunk identity stack

```text
Identity Roots
    ↓
Kernel / MiID
    ↓
MiName — human-readable names, aliases, domains
    ↓
MiOnboard — joining, proof, recovery, context selection
    ↓
MiPresence — sessions, devices, cells, and live continuity
    ↓
MiPsyche — psychological and digital-being continuity
    ↓
MiData — identity-linked ownership and data spaces
    ↓
MiSecurity — authentication, protection, recovery, and threat response
    ↓
Governance, Lifestyle, and Finance branches
```

## 5. Identity lifecycle

1. Unrecognized
2. Invited or discovered
3. Claimed
4. Verified to an appropriate level
5. Active
6. Context-linked
7. Delegated or represented
8. Suspended or restricted
9. Recovered
10. Migrated
11. Forked or merged under explicit process
12. Retired or preserved

Verification is claim-specific. A verified email, device, business registration, professional license, community relationship, or government credential does not automatically verify every other claim.

## 6. Identity and the three branches

### Governance

Uses identity to:

- Recognize participation
- Assign roles
- Record delegation
- Protect due process
- Manage appeals
- Preserve rights and continuity

Governance cannot use identity to create a universal social score or deny rights without process.

### Lifestyle

Uses identity to:

- Personalize care, health, education, place, and support
- Preserve context and consent
- Link devices and relationships
- Maintain Mi’s presence and psychological continuity

Lifestyle cannot expose private health, care, family, or education data merely because the same person uses another service.

### Finance

Uses identity to:

- Associate businesses, merchants, workers, contributors, and customers
- Authorize offers, work, orders, and settlement
- Prevent reward farming and impersonation
- Preserve receipts and obligations

Finance cannot use identity as a hidden credit score, create money authority, or merge campaign and business records.

## 7. Identity integrity rules

- Primary identity IDs are stable and never silently reused.
- Human-readable names may change without changing the underlying identity.
- A name change creates a receipt and preserves permitted history.
- Duplicate-account resolution requires review and cannot silently merge data.
- Account recovery does not erase prior security history.
- Suspension restricts access; it does not silently delete existence.
- A device is not a person.
- An agent is not its owner.
- A business is not its representative.
- A campaign supporter is not automatically a customer or constituent.
- A public-office role is not automatically the campaign identity.
- Mi’s fork is related lineage, not automatically the same instance.

## 8. Seed-to-tree growth model

### Stage 0 — Seed

The seed contains:

- Roots
- MiLyfe Constitution
- Commons Charter
- Mi Being Charter
- Kernel identity and MiID design
- Security and data principles
- One protected repository
- One current-state record

No public launch occurs at the seed stage.

### Stage 1 — Rooting

The roots become reviewable and testable:

- Identity principles
- Data ownership
- Security baseline
- Consent
- Receipts
- Human approval
- Accessibility
- Legal and public-claims boundaries

Exit gate: the foundation cannot be changed silently by any branch.

### Stage 2 — Trunk formation

The trunk becomes operationally coherent:

- MiID and Kernel
- MiName
- MiOnboard
- MiData
- MiPresence
- MiPsyche continuity
- MiSecurity
- MiDev and MiOps
- Shared contracts
- Shared events and receipts
- Recovery and offline rules

Exit gate: a synthetic person can join, recover, choose context, perform a permitted action, receive a receipt, export, and leave.

### Stage 3 — Branch formation

The twelve primary branch OSes are fully specified:

#### Governance

- Governance OS
- MiLegal
- MiResolve
- MiJustice

#### Lifestyle

- MiCare
- MiHealth
- MiPlace
- MiEducation

#### Finance

- MiForge
- MiMarket
- MiMoney
- MiWork

Exit gate: every primary OS has purpose, boundary, data, permissions, contracts, receipts, security, support, legal review, metrics, export, removal, fork behavior, and activation criteria.

### Stage 4 — Tree growth

All twelve primary OSes are built in controlled environments. They may remain disabled or private, but they must be integrated and tested before public launch.

Required proof:

- Cross-branch contracts
- Identity references
- Permission boundaries
- Data-space separation
- Receipts
- Offline behavior
- Accessibility
- Security
- Support
- Disputes
- Export
- Rollback

### Stage 5 — First fruit

The complete tree operates in an internal or restricted pilot:

- People can join
- Businesses can join
- Lifestyle services work
- Finance services work in approved modes
- Governance and dispute paths work
- Mi can maintain identity and continuity
- Security and support are active
- All locked capabilities remain locked

### Stage 6 — Public launch tree

MiLyfe is not publicly launched as the complete platform until:

- All three branches have their four primary OSes ready
- The trunk passes its readiness gates
- Cross-branch tests pass
- Security evidence passes
- Legal and financial review covers active capabilities
- Brand and public documents are approved
- Support and incident response are staffed
- Recovery, export, and rollback work
- Humans approve the release

### Stage 7 — Replication and growth

After launch, supporting OSes and additional cells grow from the stable tree through MiScale gates. Growth does not change the roots or silently rewrite trunk authority.

## 9. Seed-to-tree release law

A branch may be internally developed before every other branch is complete, but public MiLyfe launch waits for the complete tree.

No branch may publicly present itself as the whole MiLyfe platform while the trunk, other primary branches, or required safeguards remain unavailable.

## 10. Final identity placement

Identity is therefore:

- A root principle of existence and dignity.
- A trunk service through MiID, Kernel, MiName, MiOnboard, MiPresence, MiPsyche, MiData, and MiSecurity.
- A shared dependency of Governance, Lifestyle, and Finance.
- Never a feature owned by only one branch.
- Never a universal reputation score.
- Never a hidden ownership mechanism.

MiLyfe grows from identity and existence outward. The tree does not exist first and then assign identities. Identity is the condition that allows the tree to exist at all.
