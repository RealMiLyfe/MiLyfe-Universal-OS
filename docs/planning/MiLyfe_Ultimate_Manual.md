# PART TWO — $MLY LEGAL FRAMEWORK

## What $MLY Is

A community credit issued by public rules within the MiLyfe platform. Real from day one. No CEO. No founder mint. No APY. No "investment in our team."

## What People Can Do With $MLY (From Day One)

**Earn it:**
- UBI: weekly credits to every verified human member
- Quests: complete real community tasks (verify resources, teach, clean, care)
- Contributions: care, peace, teach, keep, build, consented sensor operation
- Circulation bonuses: small, capped bonuses for accepting and spending locally

**Spend it:**
- Marketplace purchases (goods, services, food, rides)
- Shop payments (any shop that chooses to accept)
- Thank someone by name
- Community project donations
- Circle treasury contributions

**Trade it (voluntary, legal, from day one):**
- Peer-to-peer swap with anyone who consents
- Cash, BTC, goods, food, labor — anywhere two people agree
- No permission needed from MiLyfe or anyone else
- The protocol does NOT geo-fence a peer payment

**Save it:**
- Personal jars (goals, emergency fund)
- Household shared jars (rent, food, kids)
- Community pots (place treasury, circle treasury)

## What MiLyfe Does NOT Do

- Does not run an exchange (no order book, no market-making, no listing)
- Does not custody other people's money (your credits, your keys, your export)
- Does not sell $MLY (no ICO, no token sale, no investor allocation, no pre-mine beyond UBI)
- Does not pitch $MLY as an investment (no APY, no yield, no dividends, no appreciation promise)
- Does not set the price (peer swaps happen at whatever rate two people agree to)
- Does not restrict peer-to-peer trading between consenting adults

## The Peer-Swap Legal Basis

Two people can voluntarily exchange anything they both consent to. This is basic commerce and barter, legal everywhere.

If someone runs a public cash-out booth or exchange desk, THEY are the exchange. They follow MSB/MiCA/local rules. MiLyfe does not operate that booth. We may list self-declared community doorways (places that accept $MLY) — listing is not operating.

## What Requires Community Vote + Legal Review to Activate

- Public exchange listing (Uniswap, CEX, etc.)
- Cross-border automated exchange infrastructure
- Merchant POS integration with fiat settlement

## Legal Nature (Said Plainly)

$MLY is a community credit. It is not a security (no expectation of profit from others' efforts). It is not a commodity (no underlying asset). It is not a currency (not issued by a government). It is a voluntary medium of exchange within a community — same legal category as arcade tokens, airline miles, or co-op store credit — except members can freely trade them peer-to-peer because we don't restrict that right.

## Issuance Rules

- **Weekly UBI:** every verified human member. Not shops. Not helpers. Not extra cards. Kids: gift-only until money-age.
- **Contribution:** care, peace, teach, keep, build, consented sensors. Local first.
- **Circulation:** small, capped bonuses for accepting and spending locally and teaching the pocket.
- **NEVER:** founder cut, investor allocation, HODL contest, referral pyramid, buying a right.
- **Default pot split:** 70% place / 30% commons (so Jacksonville is not drained by distant farms).
- **Earn caps per week** (so whales cannot eat UBI).

## Safeguards

- 34% treasury circuit breaker: spending over 34% of total $MLY triggers 48-hour cool-down + 80% supermajority required
- No deprivation: no ledger debit that would cause negative balance
- Freeze: safety freeze available for abuse situations (leave-now)
- Export: full transaction history exportable at any time, even during disputes
- Three pots visible: Weekly (yours) / Thanks (what you received) / Place (community)

# PART 5D — WORD-TO-MATH ENGINE & ON-DEVICE AI

## Word-to-Math (Natural Language → Executable Formulas)

Members speak in plain language. The system converts to executable actions.

**Example:** "Allocate 150 $MLY for Circle community garden seeds and soil"

**Becomes:**
```json
{
  "action": "ALLOCATE",
  "amount": 150,
  "asset": "MLY",
  "target": "Circle community garden",
  "charterCompliant": true,
  "review": { "verdict": "requires-circle-vote", "impact": "economic" }
}
```

### How It Works
1. Member types or speaks a request in natural language
2. System parses → generates Formula AST (Abstract Syntax Tree)
3. Constitutional compliance check runs automatically
4. Safety guards enforce: no-deprivation (can't go negative), charter alignment
5. AST displayed as human-readable "Formula Card" for inspection
6. Requires explicit signature before execution
7. Executed on ledger only after approval

### Safeguards
- Every formula requires explicit human signature (never auto-executes money)
- Constitutional screening on every action
- No-deprivation rule: no ledger debit that would cause negative balance
- Formula breeding possible (combine two formulas → hybrid) but requires Circle vote

## Season Method (How Development Cycles Work)

1. MiNeed listens for 14 days (what's missing, what's hurting)
2. MiStory publishes the weather (no names — just patterns)
3. MiPlacePlan writes one-page season: "This season: Learn X, Safe Y, Share Z"
4. Street keepers accept or rewrite
5. MiGit opens tickets ONLY for that page
6. MiHeal applies what is safe. Humans apply the rest.
7. Season ends. Story says what changed. Repeat.

**Jacksonville Season 1:** Learn (rights, GED, parenting) + StreetGoods (one bakery, one pantry) + Reentry pack + Pulse on one block.

# PART THIRTEEN — PROFILES

One profile system. Tabs change by kind.

## Person
Photo, name, languages, skills offered, help needed, accessibility, "how to pronounce me," home place, standing words chosen to show, shop badges if working somewhere.

## Youth
Profile through a grown-up. Gifts into a watched pocket. No UBI until money-age. No adult DMs. No public location. Safety always accessible.

## Household
Members, kids (hidden from strangers), shared jar, chores if they want them, steward.

## Shop (First-Class Product)
- Pin on the STREET (not a global feed — local first)
- Hours, ramp/step accessibility, languages
- Whether they take $MLY / cash / card
- Today's surplus (food that will spoil → neighbors)
- Hire board: neighbors first, then town
- Complaint button (MUST be answered in 7 days or standing says "doesn't answer")
- Community day toggle (tools, water, shade, charging)
- Staff list with person cards
- Books: simple till, dual price, tax export

## Place
Charter, pot, hello-day calendar, radios on roofs, who's on call for Rue, languages, child policy (cannot be weaker than MiChildGate).

## Helper
Name, job, who voted them Kin, ratings, "what I cannot do," mute button.

# PART FOURTEEN — PERSONHOOD

For weekly UBI, not for existing on the platform.

## Three Paths (Any One Works)

1. Three members in good standing introduce you (web of trust)
2. In-person "hello-day" at a library / shop / church (staff stamps)
3. Optional government ID (never required for the basic profile)

Kids: profile through a grown-up. No UBI until money-age.

## Life Events

- Lost phone → recovery friends + delay
- Stolen phone → panic freeze
- Move → street-known standing cools, craft/care can travel
- Abuse split → hide, freeze, do not auto-transfer
- Death → steward takes over, UBI stops, memory page if wanted
- Leave → export card + pocket + standing + history, profile closes
- Ban from a place → you keep the global profile; that street cannot see you; appeal available

## Zero Recovery Friends

Street keeper + delay. Still YOUR profile, not a company reset.
