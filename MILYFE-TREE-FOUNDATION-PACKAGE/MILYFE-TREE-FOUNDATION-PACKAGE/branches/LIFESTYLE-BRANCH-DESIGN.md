# MiLyfe Lifestyle Branch Design

**Status:** Focused design phase
**Primary OSes:** MiCare, MiHealth, MiPlace, MiEducation

## 1. Branch purpose

The Lifestyle Branch serves daily human life: care, health, local place, learning, belonging, and personal development.

## 2. MiCare

Owns care coordination, caregiver relationships, disability support, elder care, child care, respite, supported decisions, and care-network planning.

It does not diagnose, prescribe, or replace licensed clinical care.

## 3. MiHealth

Owns health-service boundaries, health information permissions, clinical integrations, public-health coordination, and professional-care routing.

It does not turn general AI into an unlicensed clinician.

## 4. MiPlace

Owns local service coordination for housing, food, energy, water, transportation, repair, emergency resources, and community projects.

It does not replace government, emergency authorities, utilities, courts, or licensed providers.

## 5. MiEducation

Owns learning paths, skills, courses, mentorship, credentials, training, and lifelong development.

It does not create universal reputation scores or make credential claims without issuer authority.

## 6. Branch contracts

Lifestyle receives identity, data spaces, permissions, presence, agents, devices, communication, security, and support from the trunk. It returns service records, care or learning plans, place events, accessibility needs, and receipts.

## 7. Branch risks

- Health overclaim
- Child or vulnerable-person harm
- Privacy leakage
- Caregiver abuse
- Unequal access
- Emergency confusion
- Credential fraud
- Commercialization of sensitive needs

## 8. Design gates

- Human support exists
- Accessibility is tested
- Sensitive data is separated
- Professional boundaries are clear
- Emergency escalation is defined
- Consent and revocation work
- Export and deletion work
- AI cannot replace reserved human decisions
