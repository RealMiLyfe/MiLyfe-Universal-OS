# MiLyfe Commons Charter

**Status:** Draft for human and legal review
**Purpose:** Define the people-owned commons layer of MiLyfe.
**Legal boundary:** This charter describes internal commitments. It does not create a corporation, state, sovereign government, trust, cooperative, or legal person without a separate lawful structure.

## 1. The commons

MiLyfe is a shared network of people, communities, businesses, devices, agents, knowledge, services, and cells. The commons includes the shared rules, contracts, records, interfaces, and community infrastructure that make participation possible.

## 2. Stewardship

Stewardship is responsibility, not private ownership of every participant. Stewards maintain systems, explain decisions, preserve records, protect people, and transfer responsibility when their role ends.

## 3. Participation

Participation is voluntary and must be informed, accessible, reversible where feasible, and free from hidden coercion. Every participant can understand the active context, authority, permissions, data, value state, support path, and exit path.

## 4. Shared protections

The commons protects:

- Human dignity
- Privacy
- Accessibility
- Equal participation
- Security
- Consent
- Due process
- Data portability
- Community self-determination
- Transparent economic rules
- Honest AI and digital-being representation

## 5. Anti-capture

No person, founder, business, agent, vendor, cell, treasury, or security function may silently acquire permanent control of the commons. Authority is scoped, recorded, reviewable, and transferable.

## 6. Contributions

People may contribute work, knowledge, money, devices, infrastructure, care, creativity, or stewardship. Contributions require a defined purpose and cannot be converted into ownership or reward without an approved rule.

## 7. Shared infrastructure

Shared infrastructure must be documented, secured, exportable, recoverable, and replaceable. A service can be replaced without erasing the people, records, or value relationships it served.

## 8. Disagreement and repair

The commons supports discussion, correction, mediation, appeal, legal routing, rights defense, and restitution where appropriate. Retaliation against good-faith criticism or reporting is prohibited.

## 9. Fork and departure

A cell or community may leave, restrict a bridge, migrate, or fork according to the Kernel, data, money, and governance rules. Departure must not erase ownership or history.

## 10. Amendment

Amendments require public explanation, impact review, accessible participation, defined approval, versioning, and a transition plan. Changes cannot silently remove knowledge, appeal, export, or exit rights.

## Adoption

This charter remains a draft until adopted through the ratification procedure and reviewed for applicable law, accessibility, security, and community impact.
