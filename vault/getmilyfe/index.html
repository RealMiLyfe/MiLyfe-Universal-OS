Quick questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Joining costs $0 and core citizenship stays free. The platform itself was built for $0 with open-source tools — that's the proof behind the whole project.

Do I have to live in Jacksonville?

Jacksonville goes first — the full street-level experience is live there. Anyone can join, follow along, and help build while their city waits its turn.

What is $MLY?

The community share. Citizens receive it weekly, earn more by contributing, and spend it with neighbors and local businesses. The money stays on the street.

Who owns MiLyfe?

The community — 100%. Open source, public ledger, removable helpers. A system that still works after anyone leaves.

I'm not political. Is this for me?

MiLyfe had never voted before age 40. This isn't left or right — it's neighbors. Help people, get your share, have a say. That's the whole pitch.
