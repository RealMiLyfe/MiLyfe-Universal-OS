Our Promise

Home/Our Promise

Add Value. Leave Power With the People.

This is we, us, our. Politicians have always promised, served their own intent, and left — then a new face inherited the same broken system. We are building the first system that still works after anyone leaves.

1. The office executes — the people decide

Willing people from any party can help in office. One hundred percent of the power stays with the people. Helpers who stop serving can be removed.

2. Prevention over reaction

We will move money from the most expensive possible approach — waiting for harm, then reacting — toward prevention that pays: peace treaties, guild economics, second chances. A good neighbor must be worth more than a bad one. Economics, not speeches.

3. Open books, open code

Don't take a speech. Look at milyfe.fun and GitHub. The budget, the receipts, the code — public. If you can't inspect it, you don't own it.

4. $0 excuses

This entire platform was built for $0 with free, open-source tools. "We can't afford to fix it" is no longer an acceptable sentence in Jacksonville government.

How You'll Hold Us To It

Public ledger

Every dollar in, every dollar out — published where anyone can read it.

You-said / we-did

Citizen input goes in; the record shows what the office did about it. No black box.

Removal, not hopes

Helpers serve at the pleasure of the people — with real mechanisms, not vibes.
