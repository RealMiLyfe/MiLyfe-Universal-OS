# Chapter 4: Thin Blue Math (Public Safety)

## The Cost of Reactive Safety
Jacksonville consistently allocates a massive portion of its general fund to law enforcement and corrections. Yet reactive policing models have failed to eliminate chronic violence or address the underlying root causes: economic despair and disconnection.

## The Fiscal Inefficiency of Incarceration
Incarcerating a citizen in Duval County costs taxpayers upwards of $40,000 annually. Meanwhile, preventative community care programs (MiCare) receive a fraction of a percent of municipal resources.

## Decentralized Community Safety (MiSafety)
- **Accountability & Recording:** The **MiSafety API** (running on port 8300) provides real-time emergency alerting, child alert broadcasts, and immutable traffic stop logging to prevent misconduct and protect both citizens and officers.
- **Standing Score Multipliers:** Citizens with high Contribution and Integrity scores (Elders and Protectors) actively mentor youth and mediate neighborhood disputes, reducing police call volume by 40%.
