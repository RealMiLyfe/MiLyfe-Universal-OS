## Filing Requirements (Florida)

- **DS-DE 9:** Appointment of Campaign Treasurer and Designation of Campaign Depository
- Must be filed BEFORE collecting any contributions or making expenditures
- Filed with Duval County Supervisor of Elections
- Source: dos.myflorida.com/elections/candidates-committees
