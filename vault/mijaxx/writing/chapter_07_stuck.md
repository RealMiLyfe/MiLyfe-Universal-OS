# Chapter 7: Stuck (Transportation)

## The Transit Deficit
Jacksonville is the largest city by land area in the contiguous United States (875 square miles). Yet public transportation infrastructure is chronically underfunded, leaving working families entirely dependent on private vehicle ownership.

## The Economic Tax of the Commute
- Households in Duval County spend over 22% of their annual income on transportation (car payments, insurance, fuel, maintenance).
- Poor transit connectivity prevents residents in high-unemployment ZIP codes from accessing economic hubs.

## The MiMarket Rides & Mobility Solution
- **Decentralized Ride-Sharing:** The MiMarket module includes peer-to-peer rideshare services governed by citizens and transacted in $MLY, eliminating Uber/Lyft corporate take-rates.
- **Smart Mobility Corridors:** Integrated data from MiCity sensors optimizes traffic flow and public transit scheduling at $0 software cost.
