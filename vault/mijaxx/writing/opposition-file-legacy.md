# Opposition Research: Donna Deegan
## Accountability Brief v1.0

## STRENGTHS (Understand and Respect)
- First female Jacksonville mayor
- Breast cancer survivor - sympathetic
- Strong name recognition (TV anchor)
- Established donor network
- Democratic base support
- Incumbency advantage
- Media relationships

## RECORD TO RESEARCH (Pull from coj.net)
- FY2023-24 budget: Did she meet pension ADC?
- FY2024-25 budget: Infrastructure investment?
- Septic-to-sewer: What is current pace?
- JSO staffing: Higher or lower than 2023?
- DIA incentives: Donor-recipient correlation?
- OIG reports: Any findings on her watch?
- Veto record: What did she veto and why?

## KEY ACCOUNTABILITY QUESTIONS
1. Pension: Did she contribute 100% of ADC?
2. Septic: How many connections per year?
3. Safety: Is JSO staffing gap closing?
4. Housing: Affordability better or worse?
5. Transparency: Dashboard published?
6. Incentives: Independent ROI reporting?

- coj.net/departments/finance/budget
- pfpf.org (pension actuarial reports)
- jaxsheriff.org (annual reports)
- dos.myflorida.com (campaign finance)

## THE CONTRAST (Not Personal - Record Only)
"Mayor Deegan is a good person
with good intentions.
Good intentions are not the same as results.
Jacksonville deserves both."

## DO NOT ATTACK
- Her personal story (cancer survivor)
- Her historic significance (first female mayor)
- Her character or integrity without proof
- Anything that looks like personal attack

## DO ATTACK
- The gap between promises and delivery
- Structural problems unaddressed
- Budget math that doesn't work
- Infrastructure pace too slow
- Donor-incentive patterns (public record)
