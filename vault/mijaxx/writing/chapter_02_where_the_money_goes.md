# Chapter 2: Where the Money Goes

## Tracing Duval County Municipal Capital
Jacksonville's $1.7+ billion annual municipal budget presents a labyrinth of special funds, debt service allocations, and contractual obligations. Where do the tax dollars of 900,000 citizens actually go?

## Debt Service vs. Direct Community Care
A critical breakdown of city expenditure demonstrates that interest payments on legacy debt and developer incentives outpace direct investments in community health, homelessness diversion, and youth programs.
- **The Subsidy Loop:** Large-scale commercial developments receive tax increment financing (TIF) rebates, reducing the general fund balance available for neighborhood sidewalks, drainage, and lighting.
- **Administrative Overhead:** Traditional municipal bureaucracies consume significant capital in administrative friction, legal fees, and consulting contracts.
