- Public Safety (reaction to failure): 52-55% of general fund
- Public Health (prevention of failure): 3-4% of general fund

That means: for every dollar Jacksonville spends preventing a problem, it spends roughly $15 reacting to one.

This is the most expensive possible approach to governing a city.

- Worse outcomes mean more spending on the back end
