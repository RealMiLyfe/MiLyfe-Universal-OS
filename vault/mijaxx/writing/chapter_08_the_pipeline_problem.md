# Chapter 8: The Pipeline Problem (Education)

## From Sandbox to Sovereignty
Duval County Public Schools face budget shortfalls, teacher shortages, and standardized testing pressures that fail to equip youth for the AI-driven economy.

## The MiLearn & Kids Sandbox Commitment
- **The MiCity Youth Promise:** Every child who participates in the MiLyfe Youth Hub (from sandbox to vault) accumulates $MLY in a sovereign trust fund, graduating at 18 with real capital to buy a home or fund a business.
- **Elder Mentorship:** Highly ranked MiCity Elders earn $MLY by teaching coding, carpentry, arts, and constitutional literacy to the next generation.
- **Local AI Tutors:** Every student receives unlimited, personalized tutoring via our local Qwen 2.5 AI assistants, with zero data tracking or monetization.
