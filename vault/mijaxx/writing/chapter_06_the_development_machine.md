# Chapter 6: The Development Machine

## Who Owns the Mayoral Agenda?
For decades, municipal policy in Jacksonville has been dictated by a tight-knit coalition of real estate developers, land attorneys, and political consultants. This "Development Machine" turns municipal zoning and bonding authority into a private wealth pump.

## Opposition File Analysis
Our campaign research into existing administrations (including the Donna Deegan administration) shows a structural continuation of corporate handouts at the expense of community-led planning.
- DIA (Downtown Investment Authority) incentives frequently favor politically connected developers without binding community benefit agreements.
