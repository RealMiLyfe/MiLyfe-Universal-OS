# The Story of MiLyfe

## The Timeline

1986 (January 11): Born in Miami, Florida.
1991 (March 28): Mother passes. Age 5.
1994 (June 5): Father passes. Age 8.
Raised by family in Jacksonville after that.

2003 onward: Arrests begin. First arrest at age 17 in Jacksonville. 11 Jacksonville arrests as an adult, plus multiple in Miami.
2006-2008: Homeless in Jacksonville. Breaking into apartments to eat and shower. Surviving.
2008 onward: Criminal record accumulates. 30+ arrests total over the years.

2015: Age 29. His mother died at 29. He's scared. He has a daughter. He wants better.

2015 (June 5): THE WORK BEGINS. Exactly 21 years to the day after his father passed. Writes everything wrong about himself in a notebook. Defines "UNDERSTAND." Asks one question: "Why do people fight?"

2015-2026: 11 years of independent research. No income from this work. Not one dollar. Sold cars and car services. Sold computers. Ate from trash cans — both during survival and during the build. Bathed with a water hose at a storage unit. Lost relatives and friends who counted him out. Lost communication with his daughter's mom. Unable to pay child support. His daughter stopped asking about "Frankenstein" and only texts every blue moon. The silence was the weight. Nearly ended it all twice. Survived both times.

2026: Cuts off relatives who doubted him. "Indefinitely. Never coming back."

2025 (August 25): Brutalized by police in a crosswalk. Emergency room visit. A $2.5M civil rights demand letter is prepared and ready to file (not yet filed).

2026 (July 24): MiLyfe platform founded.
2026 (July 29): Registers to vote for the first time in his life. At 40 years old. To put his OWN name on the ballot.

## The Turning Point

"I was done. Either this is revealed to me — what this project is for, why I built it, who it serves — or I quit. I give up. I was past feeling stupid, dumb, devalued. I felt betrayed by the universe. I cursed the creator.

Then it was revealed.

And I cried. Because for the first time in 20 years as an entrepreneur, I had something with no bullshit on it. Something I could be 100% proud of. Something honest.

I can just be honest through my brokenness."

## The Campaign Line

"The city arrested me 11 times, harassed me hundreds of times. I'm running to lead it. Not for revenge. For the lost boys and girls who are still out there. Who just need someone to show them what's on the other side of the bottom."
