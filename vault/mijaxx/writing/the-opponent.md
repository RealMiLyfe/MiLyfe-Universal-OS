# Opponent Intelligence

## RULES OF ENGAGEMENT
- Never attack personally. Hold records accountable.
- Never mock strengths. Understand them.
- Never disrespect historic significance.
- Always have the source. Always cite public record.
- The contrast is: promises vs delivery. Not character vs character.

## DONNA DEEGAN (Incumbent Mayor)

### Profile
- Incumbent Mayor of Jacksonville since July 1, 2023
- Former TV news anchor (WTLV/WJXX First Coast News)
- Democrat, first female Jacksonville mayor
- Breast cancer survivor and activist (Marathon of Hope Foundation)
- Strong personal narrative and name recognition
- Established donor network

### Her Stated Priorities (from coj.net/mayor)
1. Public Safety — enhanced JSO salaries, pension stability, new fire stations, Jacksonville Journey revival
2. Infrastructure — septic elimination, stormwater, resilience, blight
3. Housing — affordable development, down-payment assistance, eviction diversion, shelter expansion
4. Health — maternal/child health, mental health, elder care, social determinants
5. Economic Development — permitting reform ($21M), small business support, downtown
6. Youth & Literacy — River City Readers (3M+ minutes), library funding, Kids Hope Alliance
7. Military & Veterans — military-friendly city initiatives
8. Arts & Culture — cultural investment
9. Budget — FY2026-27 General Fund $2.03B, CIP $586M, zero reserves used
10. Neighborhoods — "Keep Jax Cute" litter campaign, community cleanups, Jax Litter League

### Accountability Questions (The Record vs The Claims)
1. **Pension:** Did she contribute 100% of the Annual Determined Contribution? What's the unfunded liability trend under her watch?
2. **Septic:** How many connections per year vs her claim of "acceleration"? What's the actual pace to completion?
3. **Safety:** Is the JSO staffing gap (1.7/1,000) closing or widening? What are response times by zone?
4. **Housing:** Is affordability better or worse since she took office? What do the numbers show?
5. **Transparency:** Has she published a performance dashboard? Can a citizen see where their taxes go in real-time?
6. **Incentives:** Any documented correlation between campaign donors and DIA/REV grant recipients?

### Strengths (Respect These)
- First female mayor — historic
- Cancer survivor — sympathetic, genuine
- Media relationships — knows how to communicate
- Incumbency advantage — resources, visibility, organization
- Strong base of Democratic voters

### Vulnerabilities (Record-Based Only)
- Gap between promise and delivery (measure claims vs data)
- Structural budget problems largely unaddressed
- Infrastructure pace too slow (55K septic tanks at current rate = decades)
- Pension liability still growing
- Per capita spending below all peer cities
- Council relations strained (Republican supermajority)
