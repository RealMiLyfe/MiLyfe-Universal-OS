# Chapter 9: The River Doesn't Lie (Environment)

## St. Johns River & Flood Resiliency
Jacksonville is an aquatic city defined by the St. Johns River and its tributaries. Yet wetland dredging, bulkhead erosion, and poor stormwater management threaten neighborhoods with rising flood risks.

## The True Cost of Environmental Degradation
When corporate polluters discharge effluents into local waterways, taxpayers pay the cleanup bills while property values along tributaries stagnate.
