The platform

Not a slogan. A system.

MiLyfe is a working civic platform — already built, already running, already governed by its users. This is what the campaign is running on.

One account

Everything a citizen needs, in one place.

Free, offline-capable, and private by design. Your data belongs to you — enforced at the database, not just promised in the UI.

💰

Wallet & economy

Earn 100 $MLY every Monday, claim a 50 $MLY welcome grant, and move credits around the community. Atomic transfers — no double-spending, ever.

🏛️

Community treasury

A Jacksonville baseline of $5.3B — reconciled to real grants and real transactions only. Public receipts for every cent.

🗳️

Governance

Proposals move Idea → Talk → Try it first → Decide → What happened. Vote directly or delegate — time-limited, topic-specific, instantly revocable.

🛒

The street

Your neighborhood: marketplace, quests, surplus food that will spoil, rides with room for two, and care exchange — trade hours, not money.

🎓

Learn — free forever

Ten paths: rights and papers, parenting, reentry, peace, food and first aid, repair, money that is not a casino, literacy, the trade this place lacks, and how to run a street. Badges leave with you.

🛡️

Safety & justice

One-tap leave-now, walking-home, freeze. An encrypted journal only you can read — the server stores ciphertext. Public rights guides, situation by situation.

💬

Mi

An ambient assistant with named personalities — "Nia here, your credits just arrived." It does things, shows its sources, and says "I don't know" when it does.

📰

News & community

A community feed, forum, and a wiki the people maintain. Your story only if you tap in — never scraped, never sold.

🌐

Offline-first

Works on a phone, a kiosk, or nothing at all. Local-first data, queues that replay, and a fallback page when the network disappears.

Built to survive

The security posture is part of the product.

Private by law

108 row-level-security policies. Your data is only accessible to you and the people you explicitly share it with — enforced in the database itself.

Public by proof

35 routes, 48 tables, 14 versioned migrations. Every action leaves a viewable receipt. The code is AGPL — anyone can read exactly how it works.

Resilient by design

Server-side encryption, sanitized input, fail-safe auth. A device is a node: the site is the routing layer, people's devices do the lifting.
