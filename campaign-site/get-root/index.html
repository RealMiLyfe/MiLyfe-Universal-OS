Three steps

You are a citizen in 60 seconds.

1️⃣

Sign up

One account. No ID required to start, no address required to join. A person in any nation can create an account and use the whole platform immediately.

2️⃣

Claim your welcome grant

50 $MLY land in your pocket the moment you join. Then 100 every Monday — the weekly stipend that shows the economy works.

3️⃣

Govern from day one

Read a proposal, vote, or delegate your vote on a topic you care about. Your standing grows as you show up. The power is yours — revocable, yours.

What you get

One account. A whole city's worth of tools.

💰

Your pocket

Earn, spend, and transfer $MLY. Weekly stipend, welcome grant, and a community treasury built on receipts — not promises.

🗳️

Your voice

Proposals, hard votes, and time-limited delegations. Tap any rule and see the vote that made it — and how to change it.

🛒

Your street

Marketplace, quests, surplus, rides, and resources for your neighborhood — care and trade, not just commerce.

🎓

Free learning

Ten paths — rights and papers, reentry, parenting, repair, how to run a street. Always free, always offline-capable, never paywalled.

🛡️

Safety, one tap

Leave-now, walking-home, freeze — plus an encrypted safety journal that only you can read. Safety is not earned.

💬

Mi, your helper

An ambient assistant that does things — drafts quests, finds rides, reminds you about deadlines. Honest: shows sources, says "I don't know" when it does.

Anyone, anywhere can sign up from day one — not US-only, not invite-gated, not "coming to your country later." MiLyfe is universal from the first day it opens.
