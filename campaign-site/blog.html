The $0 proof

Corporate-equivalent build cost: about $3 million. Actual cost: $0 and 11 years. Free tools, open-source code, one machine that was paid for by selling personal property. What the receipts show

The work was built in the open and timestamped on a blockchain in 2017 — before the platform had a name, before it had a city. YuRight became MiCity. MiCity became MiJaxx. MiJaxx became MiLyfe. The name kept changing; the mission never did. Every receipt is public — verify it yourself, don't take our word for it.

The argument

Why prevention pays more than crime

Jacksonville spends 52–55% of a $5.3B budget reacting, and 3–4% preventing. That is the most expensive approach a city can buy: cages over people. A good neighbor is worth more than a bad one — that is not sentiment, that is economics. Peace Treaties, Guild Economics, Second Chances, Birthday Vault: the four programs that already run in the platform.

The story

From trash cans to City Hall

First registered to vote ever — at 40 — to put his own name on the ballot. Eleven years of independent research with no income, built while eating from trash cans and selling cars to survive. Opponents will find the arrest record. We disclosed it first. You can't attack a man who already told you everything and built something beautiful anyway.

The platform
