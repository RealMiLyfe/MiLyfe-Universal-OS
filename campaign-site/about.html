    Arrests begin — first at 17 in Jacksonville. 11 Jacksonville arrests as an adult, plus multiple in Miami.
  • 2006–2008
    Homeless in Jacksonville. Breaking into apartments to eat and shower.
  • 2008+
    The record accumulates — 30+ arrests total.
  • ~2015
    Age 29, nervous about mortality — his mother died at 29. Has a daughter. Wants better.
  • 2015 (Jun 5)
    STARTS THE WORK — 21 years to the day after his father died. Writes everything wrong about himself in a notebook. Defines "UNDERSTAND." Asks: "WHY DO PEOPLE FIGHT?"
  • 2015–2026
    11 years of independent research. No income from this work. Sold cars and computers to survive. Ate from trash cans. Bathed with a water hose. Lost relatives and friends who counted him out. Daughter stopped asking about Frankenstein. Unable to pay child support. Nearly ended it all twice.
  • 2025 (Aug 25)
    Brutalized by police in a crosswalk. Emergency room. A $2.5M demand letter, ready to be filed.
  • 2026
    Cuts off relatives who doubted him. "INDEFINITELY. NEVER COMING BACK."
  • 2026 (Jul 24)
    MiLyfe founded.
  • 2026 (Jul 29)
    REGISTERS TO VOTE — first time, at age 40 — to put his own name on the ballot. 165 days to qualifying.

The turning point

"I was done. Either this is revealed to me — what this project is for, why I built it, who it serves — or I quit. I give up. I was past feeling stupid, dumb, devalued. I felt betrayed by the universe. I cursed the creator.

Then it was revealed.

And I cried. Because for the first time in 20 years as an entrepreneur, I had something with no bullshit on it. Something I could be 100% proud of. Something honest.

I can just be honest through my brokenness."
