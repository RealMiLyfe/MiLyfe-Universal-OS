The Promise

Prevention pays more than crime.

This is not a platform of promises. Every item below is already running in the MiLyfe platform — you can log in and watch it work.

The core argument

Jacksonville's budget runs about $5.3 billion a year. Public safety — reaction — takes 52–55% of it. Public health — prevention — takes 3–4%. That is the most expensive possible approach a city can buy: cages over people.

The MiLyfe alternative costs $0 to build, because it is already built. It runs on a simple economic truth:

A good neighbor is worth more than a bad one. That is not sentiment. That is economics.
What we will run

Four programs, live in the platform today.

🕊️

Peace Treaties

Documented, verified agreements between neighbors — with verified peace and good conduct actually rewarded. No pay for territory, no "protection." Exit pathways that work: work, art, sport, parenting.

🛠️

Guild Economics

Skills organized around the street: repair, food, care, trades. A guild hall on the block — hire boards, trade hours, care exchange. Money that is not a casino: credits for life, not speculation.

🚪

Second Chances

Day-one pocket for people just out. Real ID help. Job boards that put neighbors first. No new-harm windows hidden — calendar with dates, work weeks, and a record the person owns.

🎂

Birthday Vault

A small, certain, public moment of good for every kid. The cheapest line item in the budget and the one that compounds most — because prevention is what this whole argument is about.
